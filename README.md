# Motion cues

Motion cues is an Android app that shows an overlay while you use your phone in a car, bus, or train.

It works similarly to vehicle motion cues on iPhone and aims to reduce motion sickness by drawing dots that drift with the vehicle's motion.

You can turn the overlay on manually, or enable "Start in a moving vehicle" in Customize to have it activate and deactivate automatically. A Quick Settings tile is also available.

## Customization

The Customize screen lets you adjust, similarly to Android's own Motion Assist feature:

- **Dot placement** — dots near the edges only (default), a couple of columns hugging the left/right edges (matching Android's shipped Motion Assist layout), or spread across the full screen
- **Shape** — circle, diamond, or a streaking "meteoroid"
- **Color** — pulled from your device's Material You theme (primary/secondary/tertiary)
- **Opacity**
- **Randomize** — cycles shape and color every few seconds
- **Auto-start** — requires the "Physical activity" permission, used only to detect vehicle motion
